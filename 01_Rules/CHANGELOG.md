# Rules Changelog
Newest first. Every entry cites the decision(s) that caused it.

## v0.3 — 2026-10-22 (draft)
- **Base pushes on a win** (only the Play bet pays 1:1) — D-010.
- **No river fold:** a player who has not raised by the river must bet 1× — D-010.
- **Dealer House Way H** replaces the v0.1/v0.2 house way — D-008.
- Math summary added (≈ 4.6% house edge under optimal play; EXP-003/004).

## v0.2 — 2026-10-21
- **Settlement change (D-006):** a tie now counts as a lost hand rather than an automatic overall loss. Win + Tie and Tie + Win are now PUSH (were LOSS). All other outcomes unchanged.
- Updated overview, settlement definition, example 3 (now a push) and open questions A1–A2.

## v0.1 — 2026-10-21
- First formal rules spec, converted from the original concept draft (`archive/Ultimate_Texas_Omaha_Concept.docx`).
- No substantive rule changes from the draft. Restructured into numbered sections; added Appendix A (open questions, including board-play ties).
- Reflects decisions D-001 through D-005.
