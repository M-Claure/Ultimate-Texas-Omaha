## v0.2 — 2026-10-21
- **Settlement change (D-006):** a tie now counts as a lost hand rather than an automatic overall loss. Win + Tie and Tie + Win are now PUSH (were LOSS). All other outcomes unchanged.
- Updated overview, settlement definition, example 3 (now a push) and open questions A1–A2.
